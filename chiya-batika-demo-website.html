About

A small tea room with a big heart

Chiya Batika is a newly opened tea room and cafe on Siddhartha Marg in Ghattekulo, Kathmandu. Drop in for a slow cup of chiya, a quick coffee, or a light bite with friends — simple, fresh and made to order.
